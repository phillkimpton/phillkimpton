## Phill Kimpton

Security consultant by trade, cyber nerd by nature, AI tinkerer at night.

The day job is the hardest part of vulnerability management: getting the right person to own a finding and close it. A scanner flags a CVE, the ticket lands in a queue, and it sits there, because the finding was never made undeniable and the owner was never named. That gap is where the risk actually lives, and it is the problem I have built my work around.

I design the method and the tooling that turn a raw finding into an owned decision: proof it is real, the person accountable for it, and a defensible record of what was decided. I build in Python, in focused tools rather than heavy platforms, because the person using them has a job to do and no patience for ceremony.

Most of it is private client work. The public shelf here is deliberately small.

### What I do
- Vulnerability triage and accountability, from CVE notification to closure.
- Verifying claims before anyone acts on them: from a vendor, a document, or an AI.
- Security automation that still asks a person before it sends, deletes, or commits.
